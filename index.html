How a skid package gets built

A one stop shop for the entire project.

Custom manufactured skid packages for air, gas, liquid, steam, refrigeration, chemicals, and much more. Hart lists every one of these stations as an in-house service.

  1. 3D modeling and design/build

    Multi-software 3D design, submittal drawings and seismic certifications.

  2. Custom frame fabrication

    Angle, wide flange beam, channel and box tube, cut and welded in house.

  3. Pipe spool fabrication

    Stainless, black steel, copper, PVC and sanitary tubing, by ASME Section IX certified welders.

  4. Assembly

    All the space needed for component storage, assembly and testing, and mass system production.

  5. Pressure testing

    Pressure testing is part of the package, not a separate vendor.

  6. Painting and finishing

    In-house painting, with powder coating and anodizing available.

  7. Electrical and controls

    Wiring and control installation done by the same team.

  8. FAT support and delivery

    Factory acceptance testing support, project management, and shipping through Hart’s freight partners, anywhere in the world.
