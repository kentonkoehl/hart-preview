About Hart

It started in a barn with a single pickup truck.

A family owned and operated business, with two generations working together, serving the community since 1983.

“In 1983 Hart Mechanical Contractors Inc. started out as most small family owned business did in our area, in the barn and with a single pickup truck. Since then we have been steadily growing and upgrading yearly.”

Today that means a fleet of over a dozen trucks and a facility in East Rockhill Township filled with modern fabricating and welding equipment.

Two generations work together to provide a safe, challenging, and rewarding environment for employees, which Hart calls the core of providing customers with the best products and services.

Our mission

“It is our mission to continue providing our customers with the best workmanship which has been our goal from day one. We have an exceptionally talented work force that strives to meet and exceed our customer’s needs and demands.”
